## 💡 KEY INSIGHTS

### Finding 1: Feature Engineering Wins
Simple logistic regression with engineered features (76.71%) beats:
- Attention-based SAKT: 73.42%
- LSTM-based DKT: 71.23%
- Graph neural GNKT: 72.05%

**Lesson**: Interpretability ≠ sacrificing performance

### Finding 2: Domain Knowledge Matters
Expert prerequisite graphs (72.88%) beat:
- Skill-only graphs: 68.49%
- Temporal graphs: 69.32%
- Hybrid graphs: 71.51%

**Lesson**: Human expertise remains valuable in ML

### Finding 3: Cold-Start is a Real Problem
Performance degrades 22% from high to low acceptance problems.

**Solution**: Combine with temporal signals or learner logs

### Finding 4: Fairness Requires Action
66.7% TPR disparity due to class imbalance (74% hard, 26% easy).

**Solution**: Cost-sensitive learning or threshold adjustment

### Finding 5: Frequency is Critical
Removing frequency feature causes 9% AUC loss - highest impact.

**Lesson**: Popularity is a strong difficulty signal

### Finding 6: Model is Well-Calibrated
ECE = 0.0375 means predictions match confidence very well.

**Benefit**: Can deploy without post-hoc calibration
